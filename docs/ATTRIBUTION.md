# Attribution and references

The FTHBF source is distributed under the [MIT License](../LICENSE). Dependencies
are installed separately and retain their own licenses. DeepMIMO scenario files
are external inputs; the source license does not grant rights to those files.

The implementation cites the following methods. These references describe the
algorithms used or adapted; they do not identify these files as the referenced
authors' official software implementations.

- `solutions/utils.py`: J. Jin, Y. R. Zheng, W. Chen, and C. Xiao,
  “Hybrid Precoding for Millimeter Wave MIMO Systems: A Matrix Factorization
  Approach,” *IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications*, vol. 17, no. 5,
  pp. 3327–3339, 2018. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TWC.2018.2810072).
- `benchmark_algorithms/AOMO_alg.py`: S. F. Kimaryo and K. Lee,
  “Downlink Beamforming for Dynamic Metasurface Antennas,” *IEEE Transactions
  on Wireless Communications*, vol. 22, no. 7, pp. 4745–4755, 2023.
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TWC.2022.3228272).
- `benchmark_algorithms/Decomp_alg.py`: H. Zhang, N. Shlezinger, F. Guidi,
  D. Dardari, M. F. Imani, and Y. C. Eldar, “Beam Focusing for Near-Field Multiuser
  MIMO Communications,” *IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications*, vol. 21,
  no. 9, pp. 7476–7490, 2022. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TWC.2022.3158894).
- Channel data: A. Alkhateeb, “DeepMIMO: A Generic Deep Learning Dataset for
  Millimeter Wave and Massive MIMO Applications,” *Information Theory and
  Applications Workshop (ITA)*, pp. 1–8, 2019.
  [DeepMIMO project](https://github.com/DeepMIMO/DeepMIMO).

The accompanying FTHBF manuscript and author list are recorded in
[`CITATION.cff`](../CITATION.cff). Publication identifiers are intentionally
omitted until they are available.
